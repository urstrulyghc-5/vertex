What Analytics is.

Analytics is the disciplined use of data, evidence, and method to understand what has happened, why it happened, what is likely to happen next, and what to do about it. It draws on mathematics, statistics, computing, and business judgement together, not any one of them alone.

It is not confined to one profession or industry. A finance analyst, a marketing manager, a logistics planner, and a researcher all use the same underlying methods toward different questions. Vertex organizes that shared body of knowledge into thirteen connected domains, from its mathematical foundations to the artificial intelligence reshaping it today.

Two places to go from here: open the universe to see the domains, or scan all concepts at once.
